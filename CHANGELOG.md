## [1.0.0] - 2026-03-18

### Added

- Multi-year timeline visualization for activities and opportunities
- Activity tracking with seller and customer participant information
- Opportunity staging display (Prospect, Qualified, Technical Validation, Launched, etc.)
- Revenue visualization normalized to the largest opportunity
- Configurable layout via `faceWidthRatio` and `activitiesHeightRatio` props
- Bound activities linked to specific opportunities and free standalone activities
- TestHarness component for visual verification and testing
